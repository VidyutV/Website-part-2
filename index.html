About Dosa

A dosa is a popular, thin, and savory crepe originating from South India. Celebrated globally for its unique crisp texture and golden-brown hue, it is a staple breakfast and comfort food enjoyed at any time of the day.

Key Characteristics

• The Batter: It is traditionally crafted from a fermented blend of rice and white urad dal (skinned black gram), oftenenhanced with a dash of fenugreek seeds to aid fermentation and deepen the golden color.

• The Texture: The batter is spread in a circular spiral motion onto a sizzling hot griddle or tawa, creating a crepe that can be tissue-thin and paper-crisp or slightly thicker and pillowy depending on regional styles.

• Accompaniments: It is universally served piping hot alongside sambar (a tangy, aromatic lentil and vegetable stew)anda variety of chutneys, most commonly fresh coconut or spicy tomato-onion chutneys.
